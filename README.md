# Hi, I’m Mukul

I work on superconducting quantum circuits as a Project Engineer at C-DAC, Bengaluru. I completed my M.Tech in Semiconductor Technology at IISc, with a minor in Quantum Technology.

My interests include circuit QED, two-qubit gates and control, readout and parametric amplification. My earlier projects include Raman and AFM studies of VO₂ wires and silicon photonic device simulation.

## Selected work

- [CR and ECR gate simulations](https://github.com/Mukul-Quantum-Researcher/Superconducting-qubits-hardware): coupled-transmon models, pulse calibration and leakage analysis in QuTiP.
- [Purcell-filter design](https://github.com/Mukul-Quantum-Researcher/purcell-filter-design): Qiskit Metal geometry, HFSS designs and exported results.
- [SNAIL JTWPA design study](https://github.com/Mukul-Quantum-Researcher/snail-jtwpa-design): Python parameter design; nonlinear circuit validation is in progress.
- [VO₂ thesis](https://mukul-quantum-researcher.github.io/projects/vo2-thesis/): Raman observations and the limits of stress transfer and AFM loading.
- [Silicon ring modulator](https://mukul-quantum-researcher.github.io/projects/ring-modulator/): optical and electrical modelling in Lumerical.
